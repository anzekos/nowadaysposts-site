The first week of the holiday rush is a good time to fix the small things you look at every day. Today's list has one Christmas piece that glows, and four that are just as useful in February.

**A Christmas village set for the mantel.** Eight pieces: three white ceramic houses with glossy gold roofs, two gold deer and snow-dusted mini trees. The houses light up from inside, so the set glows on a mantel, a sideboard or a windowsill next to a few stockings or a garland. It is a small, calm way to decorate if a full tree is too much.

**A coffee table decor set that arrives styled.** Three matte ceramic vases in rust, sage and mustard, a round dark wood tray and a wooden bead garland with a jute tassel. The tray keeps the vases together as one centerpiece, and you can add a dried stem or a candle. It works on a coffee table, a console or a dining table, in December and after.

**A small touch lamp for the nightstand.** A frosted white glass shade on a round white base, about 7 inches tall. One tap steps through three brightness levels, so it is a reading light at ten and a night light at two. It fits a bedside table, a desk or a bookshelf where a full lamp will not.

**Bathroom rugs, a set of two.** A short mat and a long runner in fog blue and white gradient stripes, made of soft chenille with a non-slip backing. Put the runner in front of the vanity and the short mat by the tub. The blue works well with white or navy cabinets.

**A ceramic canister set for the kitchen counter.** Three white embossed jars in three sizes with a natural clay-toned base and bamboo lids with a leather pull. The silicone seal keeps coffee, flour or sugar fresh, and they look good enough to leave out, which means the counter ends up with less clutter.

If you buy one thing today, make it the lamp or the bathroom rugs, since you will use them every day. The village set is the timely one, and it is worth ordering a couple of weeks before Christmas.
