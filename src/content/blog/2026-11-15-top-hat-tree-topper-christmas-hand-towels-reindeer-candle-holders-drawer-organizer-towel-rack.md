There are about ten days left before the Black Friday rush and a little over five weeks before Christmas, so this is the week when the small decor pieces are still easy to find and ship on time. Today's list is three Christmas touches that take two minutes to set up, plus two organizers that keep earning their place in January.

**A top hat for the top of the tree.** A red sequin top hat, 9 inches tall and 12 inches wide, with a white snowflake, two candy canes, a sprig of greenery and a red plaid ribbon bow whose tails fall down the branches. It sits over the top branch and works like a snowman hat for the whole tree, which suits a red and white or farmhouse look. Because it is wide, it also looks right on a smaller tree where a star can feel too small.

**Christmas hand towels for the bathroom.** A set of 2 terry hand towels in a red and black buffalo check, each with an embroidered snowman in a scarf. The bathroom is the easiest room to forget at Christmas, and a pair of towels on a ring or folded beside the sink is enough to change it. It is a good choice for a guest bathroom or a powder room, and the towels go back in the drawer in January.

**Reindeer candle holders for a table or mantel.** Three bronze metal reindeer in three heights, 8.3, 10 and 12 inches, each holding a tealight in a glass cup with a pine sprig and red berries at the neck. Stand them in a loose row on a dining table runner, a console or a windowsill. The different heights mean you do not need much else around them.

**An expandable drawer organizer for the kitchen.** A bamboo tray 2.5 inches tall that slides out to about 20 inches wide to fit your drawer. It has narrow slots for forks, spoons and knives and a wider section for spatulas and tongs, so one tray covers both the silverware drawer and the utensil drawer. Measure the inside of the drawer first and check that it sits within the range.

**A wall mounted towel rack.** A black metal frame, 32 inches tall, that holds rolled bath towels one above the other. It takes no floor space, which matters in a small bathroom, and it keeps clean towels in sight and dry next to the tub or shower. Plan the spot before you drill so the lowest towel clears the edge of the tub or a vanity.

If you only buy one thing from this list, make it the drawer organizer or the towel rack, since you will use them every day of the year. The tree topper and the reindeer are the two to order early, so they arrive before the holiday shipping delays.
