The same handful of home searches keeps climbing on Pinterest: girl closet ideas, forest themed decor, minimalist decor and small bathroom ideas. We covered them at the end of October with a rug, a lamp, a hall tree and a shower caddy, so this time each one gets a different kind of answer.

**A girl closet that stops eating jewelry.** A double sided hanging organizer with 80 clear pockets. Earrings, rings, bracelets and thin necklaces each get their own pocket, so nothing tangles and you can see what you own. It hangs from the closet rod on a metal hook or from the back of a door, and it folds flat. For a small closet or a dorm it is the cheapest way to get a drawer's worth of storage without a drawer.

**Forest themed decor that is not a rug.** A set of 6 unframed vintage botanical prints, 8x10 inches: a fern, mushrooms, a raspberry branch, chamomile, a pine branch and oak leaves on aged cream paper. Put them in six matching frames and you have a woodland gallery wall for a bedroom, a hallway or a reading corner. Dark wood frames against a green or deep neutral wall lean dark academia; light wood frames lean cottagecore.

**Minimalist decor that needs no flowers.** Three matte ceramic vases in cream, tan and terracotta, the tallest 10.4 inches. Their value is the restraint: one finish, three heights, and a few stems of dried pampas in one of them is the whole arrangement. They work on a console, a bookshelf, a coffee table or a mantel.

**Small bathroom storage that uses the wall above the toilet.** A 3 tier black metal rack, 64 inches tall, with an anti-tip design and no drilling. Towels, a basket of spare rolls and a plant fit on the three shelves, and it takes no floor space, which is the point in a rental or a half bath.

**For the Thanksgiving table.** Six mini gold pumpkins with a slot in the top, made to hold a name card upright at each seat. They are resin, so they come out again next autumn, and between dinners they sit on a mantel or a buffet as plain fall decor.

If you do one thing this week, start with the closet or the bathroom: both are the kind of fix that works the day it arrives. The prints and vases are the two that change how a room looks, and the pumpkins are the easy win before the Thanksgiving table gets set.
