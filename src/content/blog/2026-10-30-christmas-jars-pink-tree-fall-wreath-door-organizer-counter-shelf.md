Halloween is tomorrow, and Pinterest has already moved on. Christmas bathroom decor is up 300% this month, Christmas table decor ideas is up 400%, and Thanksgiving table decor is still climbing. Here are five finds: three for the next two holidays and two that earn their space all year.

**A Christmas centerpiece that is already decorated.** These frosted snowflake mason jars come in graduated sizes, each tied with twine and a sprig of pine, berries and a pinecone. Drop a tea light or LED candle inside and set them on a dining table, a mantel or a coffee table.

**The smallest way to decorate a bathroom.** A pink Christmas tree soap dispenser has a flocked velvet finish, scalloped tiers and a chrome pump. It turns a plain sink into holiday decor in a guest bathroom, kitchen or powder room, and it costs you no counter space beyond the soap you already keep there.

**A front door wreath that carries from Thanksgiving to winter.** This 24 inch fall eucalyptus wreath has layered leaves in burgundy, mustard and plum with thin eucalyptus stems. It looks full from the street, and it works just as well on an entryway wall or over a mantel.

**The door you are not using.** An over the door organizer with two mesh baskets and a row of hooks holds bags, hats, scarves, umbrellas and keys. It hooks over doors up to 1.76 inches thick with no drilling, which makes it a good fit for a bedroom, bathroom or dorm.

**More counter, without more counter.** A 2 tier kitchen counter shelf in wood and white metal gives spices, mugs or a coffee station a second level, with side hooks for a mug or a measuring cup. It suits a small kitchen, a coffee bar or a desk.

If you only do one thing this week, put the soap dispenser by the sink. It takes ten seconds, and it is the one change guests notice first.
