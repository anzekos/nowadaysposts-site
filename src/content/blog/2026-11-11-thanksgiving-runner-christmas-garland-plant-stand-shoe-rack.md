Thanksgiving is fifteen days away, so this is the last week to order for the table before shipping gets slow. After that the house turns to Christmas, and the pieces that make the biggest difference are the ones on the floor and along the mantel. Three timely picks today, and two that stay useful all year.

**A fall table runner that does the styling for you.** A 13 x 72 inch runner embroidered in maple, oak and acorn-leaf cutwork shapes in rust, olive, gold and burgundy. The edge follows the leaf outlines, so it looks finished without a centrepiece. Add two small pumpkins and a candlestick and the table is done.

**A knit tree skirt that hides the stand.** 48 inches across in thick ivory cable knit with a scalloped braided edge and two ribbon ties at the centre. It covers the stand and the cord, fits trees from about 4.5 to 7.5 feet, and is soft enough to stack presents on. Ivory also works with red, green or gold ornaments, so it survives a change of colour scheme.

**A lighted garland for the mantel or the stairs.** Nine feet of soft Norfolk pine branches with 60 warm white LEDs on a battery box with 8 modes and a timer. The wire core bends around a banister or along a fireplace, and the loop at the end hangs on a hook, so you do not need nails.

**A tall plant stand for an empty corner.** 41 inches high, 11.8 inches square, with a wood-look top and a lower shelf on a black metal frame, each shelf rated to 110 lbs. A large plant goes on the bottom and a small trailing one on top, which fills a corner beside a sofa or a window without taking floor space. Leveling feet steady it on uneven floors.

**A narrow shoe rack for a small entryway.** 16.5 inches wide and 36 inches tall, with a wood-look top for keys and a plant plus four adjustable steel mesh shelves that hold roughly 8 to 10 pairs. The mesh lets wet shoes dry, and the slim footprint fits beside a door, in a closet or in a dorm.

If you buy one thing today, make it the shoe rack or the plant stand, since you will use them every day. The table runner is the one with a deadline, and the tree skirt and garland are worth ordering before the Black Friday rush.
