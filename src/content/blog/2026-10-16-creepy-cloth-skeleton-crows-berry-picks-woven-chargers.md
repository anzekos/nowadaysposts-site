Halloween is 15 days away, and this is the week the searches turn into decorating. On Pinterest, Halloween door decorations are up 900% month over month and doubled again in the last week alone. Backyard Halloween party ideas are up 400%, easy DIY Halloween decorations are up 300%, and two quieter searches keep rising underneath: simple fall tablescapes and Christmas tree ideas for 2026. Here are five finds, one for each of them, in the order a guest would see them.

**The front door, and 400 inches of black gauze.** Creepy cloth is the cheapest way to win the street. This black gauze is 400 by 85 inches, enough to drape over a whole door frame and run along the porch rail. Cut it or tear it into ragged strips, then pin or tape it up in ten minutes. A porch light behind it shows the weave, and a pumpkin on each step finishes the look.

**The backyard, and a guest who never leaves.** A life-size skeleton is the prop everyone photographs at a party. This one is 5.4 feet tall with 8 posable joints, from the jaw and neck to the wrists, knees and ankles, and it has an aged bone finish instead of shiny white plastic. It cannot stand on its own, so sit it on a bench, lean it on a tree or prop it by the drinks table.

**The mantel, and six crows in ten seconds.** If you want a DIY look without the DIY, a row of crows is it. This pack has 6 realistic birds covered in black feathers, about 6 inches long, in three different poses. Each has a wire on its feet, so it grips a branch, a garland or the top of a picture frame. Put a few bare twigs in a jug and let half of them perch there.

**The table, and ten woven chargers.** Simple fall tablescapes are about texture, not extra stuff. This set of 10 chargers is 13 inches round, hand-woven from natural fibre in a warm honey tan, and sits under a standard dinner plate. Add a linen napkin and one mini pumpkin per place and the table is done, through Thanksgiving. They are heat-safe, so they double as trivets.

**The tree, and the trick for bare spots.** Christmas tree ideas for 2026 are up 300% already. Picks are what designers use to make a tree look full: this set has 8 sprigs about 7.5 inches long, each with red berries, frosted pine needles, a snow-tipped pinecone and a jute bow. Tuck them into the gaps, a wreath or a mug on the table.

If you only do one thing this weekend, do the door. It is the first thing anyone sees, it costs about fourteen dollars, and it comes down in two minutes on November 1.
