The holiday rush has not started yet, and Pinterest is already busy with the rooms people live in every day. Forest themed home decor, minimalist home decor and corner entryway designs are each up 300% this month, small bathroom decorating ideas are up 60%, and girl closet ideas keep showing up in the top five. Here is one find for each.

**Forest themed decor that is a rug, not a poster.** This washable forest area rug has a dense botanical print in layered greens with small gold flowers and a cream-striped border. It is about 0.2 inch thin with a non-slip backing and goes in the machine, so it can sit under a sofa, a bed or a dining table without becoming a project. If you want the forest look without painting a wall, start on the floor.

**Minimalist, with a little shape.** A white ceramic table lamp with a faceted geometric base and a beige linen shade is minimalist without being flat. It stands about 11.6 inches tall, which suits a nightstand, a shelf or an entryway table, and the LED bulb is included.

**A corner entryway design that earns the whole corner.** A corner hall tree wraps an L-shaped bench around the wall, with shoe racks underneath, open shelves in the middle and 10 double hooks for coats, bags and hats. The black metal frame has an anti-tipping device, which matters on a tall piece. It is built for the dead corner by the front door that usually holds a pile of shoes.

**Small bathroom storage that goes up, not out.** A corner shower caddy on a tension pole gives four shelves without drilling. The pole adjusts from 35 to 124 inches, the stainless steel baskets hold shampoo, a razor and a loofah on hooks, and it needs a flat ceiling to press against.

**The girl closet fix that is almost invisible.** Eight clear acrylic dividers clip onto a closet shelf up to 0.98 inch thick and keep handbags standing and sweaters in neat stacks. Because the panels are clear, the shelf looks tidy rather than fitted out, and they work just as well in a dorm or a linen closet.

If you only do one thing this weekend, pick the dead corner you walk past every day and decide what it should hold. Two of these five, the hall tree and the shower caddy, are built for exactly that spot.
