With Thanksgiving two weeks away, most Christmas decorating is still ahead, and the cheapest way to make a room feel festive is to change what you already sit on and hang things from. Two Christmas pieces today, and three that earn their place all year.

**Stocking holders that actually stay on the mantel.** A set of 4 heavy cast iron hangers with a flat bronze top and a deep hook. Anti-slip pads underneath keep them steady on a wood or stone ledge, so a filled stocking does not tip them over. They need no nails, so they also work on a shelf or a sideboard if you have no fireplace.

**Christmas pillow covers for the couch.** Four 18x18 covers: two red and black buffalo plaid and two cream ones with printed trees and a sleigh. They have a hidden zipper and go in the washing machine. Swapping covers takes five minutes and changes the whole sofa, then they fold away in January.

**A striped shower curtain for a calmer bathroom.** A 72x72 linen blend with warm taupe stripes on an oatmeal ground. The fabric is heavy enough to hang straight, and the neutral colour goes with brass fixtures and a wood vanity. It is a quick fix for a bathroom that feels flat.

**A folding storage ottoman for the entryway.** 30 inches wide with a tufted navy lid that lifts up and a 24 gallon box inside for blankets, shoes or toys. It folds flat when you are not using it and holds up to 660 lb, so it doubles as a seat at the front door or at the end of a bed.

**A wooden desk organizer with drawers.** Torched brown pine, 14 inches wide, with three removable drawers and open slots for pens, scissors and notepads. Stamps, clips and cables go in the drawers, so the desktop stays clear.

If you buy one thing today, make it the ottoman or the desk organizer, since you will use them every day. The stocking holders and pillow covers are the timely ones, and they are worth ordering a couple of weeks before Christmas.
