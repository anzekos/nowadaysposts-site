And for the **kitchen design** corner of the chart, the smallest upgrade with the biggest guest-comment ratio: a farmhouse ceramic soap dispenser duo — "HANDS" and "DISHES", carved into wood-look plaques — that retires the plastic bottles by the sink for good.
