Christmas is six weeks away and the shops are already full of it, which is a good time to pick the few pieces that change how a room looks and leave the rest. Today's list is three Christmas items that work in small spaces and two storage fixes you will still use in January.

**A metal collar instead of a fabric tree skirt.** A 28 inch cream steel ring with a thin gold diamond pattern that wraps around the base of an artificial tree and hides the stand and the cords. It comes in separate panels that clip together around the trunk, keeps its shape, and does not shed or wrinkle the way a skirt does. It looks farmhouse next to a snow flocked tree and it stores flat in a closet.

**Snowflake curtain lights for a window.** 8.2 feet of warm white LED drops with small snowflakes along the strands, a remote, 8 lighting modes and a timer. Hang them across a living room or bedroom window, along a mantel, or under a porch roof edge. Once it gets dark the strands look like falling snow, and the timer means you do not have to remember to switch them off.

**A tabletop tree for a desk, console or kitchen counter.** 24 inches tall, snow flocked, with warm LED lights, a red star, a bow, pine cones and more than 30 ornaments already on it, in a burlap pot base. It is a quick way to add Christmas where a full tree will not fit, like a dorm room, a small apartment or an office. The lights run on 3 AA batteries, which are not included.

**Under bed storage with wheels.** A two pack of 66 liter fabric bins, each 30 inches long and 7.5 inches tall, with a clear lid, a side handle and wheels so they roll out without lifting. One bin holds sweaters or spare bedding, the other shoes, and the lid keeps dust off. Measure the gap under your bed frame first, since it needs about 8 inches of clearance.

**Over the door hooks.** A black stainless steel rack with 5 round hooks that fits doors up to 1.77 inches thick and needs no drilling or glue. It turns the back of a bedroom or bathroom door into a place for coats, bags, scarves and towels, and the padded top keeps the door from getting scratched. Each hook holds about 30 lb.

If you only buy one thing, make it the over the door hooks or the under bed storage, since you will use them every day and not just in December. The curtain lights and the tree collar are the two to order early, before the Black Friday rush.
