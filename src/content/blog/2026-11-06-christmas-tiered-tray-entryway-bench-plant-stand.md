Two holidays are close enough to start planning, and the rooms guests actually see are the entryway, the bathroom and the table. Today's picks cover both: two seasonal pieces that do a lot with one placement, and three storage fixes that make a small space look intentional.

**A Christmas tiered tray for the coffee table.** An eight piece set with a small red house, a mini bottle brush tree, a knit gnome, a gold reindeer, a red mug and red and white wood beads. Everything sits on a wooden stand, so the coffee table gets one tidy cluster instead of scattered ornaments. It also works on a console or the kitchen counter, and it packs away in one box in January.

**A lighted pumpkin centerpiece for Thanksgiving.** Three glowing mercury glass style pumpkins on a 28 inch garland of maple leaves, berries and a pinecone. It runs on batteries with a timer, so there is no cord across the table, and it fits down the middle of a dining table, along a buffet or on a mantel. With the lights on at dusk it does the work of candles without the wax.

**A shoe rack bench for the entryway.** A black bamboo bench, 27.6 inches wide, with two open slatted shelves under the seat. You sit to put your shoes on and tuck them below, which keeps a narrow hallway or an apartment door clear. The slats let wet shoes air out, and it is light enough to move when you vacuum.

**A vanity tray for the bathroom counter.** A warm acacia wood tray, 11.7 inches long, with a raised edge. Soap, lotion, a jar of cotton swabs and a candle go on it, and the counter reads as one styled spot instead of five loose items. It also fits on a toilet tank, a dresser or a coffee table as a catchall.

**A corner plant stand for the plant collection.** Five round wood shelves on a black metal frame, stepped so a trailing pothos and taller plants each get light. It holds five pots in the footprint of one and is anti-tip, which matters in a corner near a window where the plants lean toward the glass.

If you pick one, take the shoe bench or the vanity tray: they change how a room works every day. The tiered tray and the pumpkin centerpiece are the quick wins for the two holiday tables that are coming.
