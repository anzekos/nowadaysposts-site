October is three days old and the search numbers have stopped being about planning. "Halloween door decorations" is up 400% month over month, "halloween door ideas" 400%, "simple fall tablescapes" 400% and "fall centerpieces for table" 300%. Quietly, "christmas tree ideas 2026" is up 400% too, which tells you how many people are already thinking about December. So here are five small moves, in the order someone meets them when they come to your house: the front door, the porch steps, the dining table, the coffee table and, for the early planners, the tree.

**The door, and the cheapest transformation of the season.** A door cover is the least effort Halloween decoration there is: one polyester banner, 71 inches tall and 35 wide, printed with a smiling ghost on a rope swing under a full moon, with jack-o'-lanterns around the bottom. It has a grommet in each corner and comes with ties, so it goes up in a minute on a front door, a garage door or a bare wall, and it washes and folds flat for next year. It costs about six dollars. Hang it flat, string a few orange lights around the frame and put two pumpkins in front. 4.6 stars from 68.

**The porch steps, and pumpkins that will not rot.** "Front porch pumpkins display" and "porch pumpkins" are both climbing, and real pumpkins are expensive and soft by the 25th. This set is twelve foam pumpkins in six shapes, the tallest about 8 inches and the smallest about 2, in orange with ribs, stripes and speckles and a curly stem on each. They are light, so the wind will move them if you do not tuck them against a step or into a basket. Cluster them unevenly, tall ones at the back, small ones tumbling down the bottom step, and put a lantern beside them. 4.4 stars from 809.

**The table, and the cloth that does most of the work.** A simple fall tablescape starts with the cloth. This one is a soft linen-cotton blend in deep rust with a white stitched grid and cream tassels along the hem, 55 by 84 inches, which suits a table for six to eight. It is wrinkle-resistant and water-resistant, so a spill beads up. Then you need very little: two candlesticks, white plates, three small pumpkins and a jug of dried grasses. 4.7 stars from 592.

**The coffee table, and a bowl that works with everything.** A dough bowl is the centerpiece people keep buying because it is never wrong. This one is paulownia wood in a warm rustic brown, 14 inches long, 6 wide and only 2 inches deep, so it is shallow and tray-like rather than deep, and it weighs about a pound. Fill it with mini pumpkins and dried oak leaves now, pinecones in November, ornaments in December. The knots and grain are natural, so no two are alike. 4.6 stars from 892.

**The tree, for anyone who plans ahead.** The 2026 tree skirt is not red and white. It is deep chocolate brown velvet, and this 48-inch one has the full version: a soft velvet circle, a wide ruffled edge and a row of bow ties down the opening that close it around the trunk. It looks right under gold, copper or cream ornaments and fits trees up to about 7 feet. If you wait until the first of December to buy one, the popular colours are usually the first to sell out. 4.6 stars from 733.

None of this takes more than an afternoon. Hang the banner, stack the pumpkins, lay the cloth, fill the bowl, and put the skirt in the closet for later. By Sunday evening the house reads as October from the door to the dining room.
