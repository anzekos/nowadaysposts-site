Halloween is packed away and the Christmas searches have taken over. On Pinterest, tinsel Christmas trees are up 300% this month, Christmas fireplace decor and Christmas porch decorating ideas are each up 200%, and "refresh my living room" is climbing faster than any of them. Here is one find for each.

**A fireplace that holds the stockings, not just the garland.** Six black metal stocking holders with a weighted flat base and a deep hook, so a full stocking hangs straight and the garland stays where you put it. If the mantel already has greenery on it, this is the cheapest way to make the fireplace look finished.

**Tinsel is back, and it is a good back.** A 72 foot roll of gold metallic tinsel garland, 3.5 inches wide, in four twisted strands that you can cut to any length. Wound around a full tree it gives that retro, nostalgic Christmas tree look, and the leftover length swags across a mantel or a banister. Tinsel catches tree lights better than almost any ornament does.

**A front porch in ten minutes.** Two frosted pine bushes, about 24 inches tall, with pinecones, red berries and 50 warm white LED lights each. A built-in timer runs them 6 hours on and 18 off, and they drop into an urn or a planter you already own. Outdoor Christmas decor ideas are up 400% this month, and this is the version that does not need a ladder.

**The living room refresh that is also a plant fix.** An 8 tier plant stand on a curved black metal frame with a 72 inch grow-light strip, 3 modes and 10 dimming levels. It holds about 10 pots in one corner, which is useful in November when the window light drops and houseplants start to sulk. It turns a dim corner into the part of the room people look at first.

**Storage that looks like furniture.** A set of two round ottomans in ribbed cream velvet with removable lids. The larger one swallows blankets, remotes or toys, and both double as a footrest, a vanity stool or an extra seat at the coffee table. If the goal is a living room that feels new without buying a new sofa, small pieces like these do most of the work.

If you only do one thing this weekend, start with the mantel or the porch, because the Christmas pins that get saved now are the ones people are still looking at three weeks from now. The plant stand and the ottomans are the two that stay in the room after the holidays are over.
