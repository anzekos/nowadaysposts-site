Decorating season has started, and the searches show which direction it is going. "Tv stand christmas decor" and "brown and gold christmas tree" are both up about 400% month over month on Pinterest, "bedroom christmas decor" is up 300%, and "refresh my living room" is one of the fastest growing home searches of the month. Here is one find for each, plus one that stays up long after the holidays.

**A mantel or TV stand dressed in one piece.** A 9 foot pre-lit garland of pine, cedar sprigs, pinecones and red berries, with 100 warm white lights. It runs on batteries, so there is no cord to hide behind a fireplace or a TV console, and the stems bend so you can drape it, swag it or wrap a stair rail. If you only buy one Christmas decoration for the living room, a garland does the most for the least effort.

**A neutral tree that does not look like a showroom.** Twelve matte taupe ornaments, 3.15 inches each, with a frosted finish, brushed gold marks and gold caps tied with a brown ribbon. Brown and gold sits well on a green tree with warm lights, and the same twelve balls also fill a wooden dough bowl on the coffee table, which is the "tabletop tree" look people keep saving.

**A front door that looks finished.** A 22 inch pre-lit wreath of soft cedar-style greenery with warm white LEDs and a long red velvet bow. It hangs on a hook or an over-the-door hanger in a minute. Because the bow does the colour work, it suits a plain door, a painted one or a brick entryway, and it is just as good indoors above a mantel or on a bedroom window.

**The living room refresh that is not Christmas.** A set of four corduroy pillow covers, 18 by 18 inches, in four greens from pale sage to forest. The patchwork stripes give a cream sofa texture without a pattern clash, and since they are covers, you reuse the inserts you already own. They go from fall into Christmas, where green is the one colour that already fits, and into spring unchanged.

**A room aesthetic upgrade for a few minutes of work.** A 33 foot faux ivy garland wired with 100 warm white LEDs and a remote with several light modes. Drape it over a headboard, along a bookshelf, around a mirror or down a dorm wall. It adds a soft green glow that reads as a styled corner, and unlike seasonal decor it stays up in January.

If you are deciding where to start, put up the garland and the wreath first, because Christmas pins saved now are the ones still being opened in December. The pillow covers and the ivy lights are the two that keep earning their place once the decorations go back in the box.
