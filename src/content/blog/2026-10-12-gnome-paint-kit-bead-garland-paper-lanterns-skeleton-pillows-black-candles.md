Halloween is 19 days away, and what people search for on Pinterest has shifted from ideas to things they can finish this week. Paint-your-own decor is up 800% month over month, Halloween party ideas are up 400% and Christmas tree ideas for 2026 are already up 300%. Here are five finds that fit those searches, from a quiet evening project to a room-filling party set.

**The craft, and six gnomes to paint.** This kit has everything in the box: 6 white ceramic gnomes about 4 inches tall, 12 pots of acrylic paint, 2 brushes and a palette. Each gnome has a different harvest detail to colour in. Paint them over an evening, then line them up on a shelf or the dinner table. 4.6 stars from 87.

**The tree, and a garland that looks old-fashioned on purpose.** This one is 8 feet 6 inches of real wood beads, each about half an inch wide, in a deep cranberry red with a soft sheen. Drape it in loose swags from the top and it works with gold, green or all-white trees. It is early, but tree ideas are already moving. 4.7 stars from 770.

**The party, and a whole wall of paper.** This set has 2 printed paper lanterns, an orange honeycomb ball, 5 tissue paper fans in orange, black and white, 3 honeycomb spiders and a 13 foot garland of bats and spiders. Everything folds flat and hangs with tape, so it is the cheapest way to fill a room. 4.9 stars from 65.

**The sofa, and two pillow covers.** The fastest Halloween upgrade is a new print on the cushions. This set of 2 is 18 by 18 inches with the same retro design on both sides: a skeleton holding a jack-o-lantern, a ghost and a haunted house on orange. Soft fabric, invisible zipper. Covers only, inserts are not included. 4.8 stars from 443.

**The mantel, and candles that never need a lighter.** Three real wax pillars in 4, 5 and 6 inch heights, each 3 inches wide, with a flickering LED flame and a melted-wax look around the rim. The remote sets a 2, 4, 6 or 8 hour timer. It needs 2 AA batteries, which are not included. 4.5 stars from 715.

If you only have an hour, hang the paper set. It changes a room faster than anything else on this list, and it all comes down in one go on November 1.
