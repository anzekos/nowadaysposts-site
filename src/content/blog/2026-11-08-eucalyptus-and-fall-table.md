With Thanksgiving under three weeks away and Christmas decor already going up, it is easy to buy only seasonal things. Today's list starts with three pieces that stay useful in January, then adds one piece for the Thanksgiving table and one for the Christmas mantel.

**A narrow console table for a short hallway.** This one is 32 inches wide and only 10.5 inches deep, with a rustic brown top on a black steel frame and an open shelf underneath. A narrow console table fits behind a sofa or along a short entryway wall, the top takes keys, a lamp and a vase, and the lower shelf holds a basket or a pair of boots so the floor stays clear.

**A washable area rug 8x10 for the living room.** A low pile rug in blue, cream and a little gold, with a non-slip backing so it stays flat under a sofa or a dining table. The abstract pattern hides everyday marks, and because it vacuums or wipes clean it suits a room with kids or pets. Large sizes are where a rug starts to pull a room together, so 8x10 is the one to look at first.

**Faux eucalyptus stems for a vase.** Six realistic greenery branches, up to 23 inches tall, with soft leaves and small green seed clusters. Put them in a tall vase on a console, a sideboard or a shelf and bend the wire stems to fill the shape. They are the easiest way to get fresh greenery in a room with no sun and no watering.

**A fall centerpiece for the Thanksgiving table.** A rustic wood box, 11.5 inches wide, filled with faux orange peonies, small pumpkins, pinecones, berries and maple leaves. It arrives arranged, so it goes straight onto a dining table, a coffee table or a kitchen island, and the earthy colours work with plain white plates and linen napkins.

**A Christmas garland for the mantel.** A 9 foot real-touch cedar garland with soft layered greenery that looks full when draped. Lay it along a fireplace mantel, a staircase banister or a door frame, and the bendable stem holds the shape. Stockings and a few candles are all it needs.

If you only buy one, pick the rug or the console table, since they change how a room works every day. The centerpiece and the garland are the timely ones, and both are worth ordering a week or two before the holiday.
