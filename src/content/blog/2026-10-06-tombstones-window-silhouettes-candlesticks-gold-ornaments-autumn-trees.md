"Fall porch decor 2026" is up 3000% month over month on Pinterest, "halloween window art" 500%, "simple fall tablescapes" 400%, "christmas tree ideas 2026" 400% and "backyard halloween party ideas" 300%. Those are three different holidays in one list, and that is the real October: people are decorating for Halloween, fall and Christmas in the same weeks. So here are five finds, one for each place the three overlap.

**The lawn, and a graveyard in one trip from the car.** This set is 6 foam tombstones, each 15 inches tall, in six carved designs: a spider web, a bat roof with a skull, a cat, skeleton hands, a pumpkin and a skull with a web. They are light, they push into the ground with the included stakes, and they look best leaning at different angles with some leaves and a few plastic bones around them. 4.3 stars from 564.

**The window, and shapes that show from the street.** This set has 4 plug-in wire-light silhouettes, each about 16 inches tall: a witch on a broom, a bat, a black cat on a crescent moon and a skull. Suction cups hold them on glass. Put them on the window that faces the road, turn the room light down and they read from the sidewalk. 4.6 stars from 571.

**The table, and four small candlesticks that carry it.** This set is 4 hand-painted wooden holders in distressed orange, red, olive and white, each about 3.5 inches tall, with a metal cup inside for a standard taper. Candles are not included. Run them down a linen runner with three small pumpkins in between and you have a fall table without a centerpiece. 4.5 stars from 461.

**The tree, and one colour that makes it look planned.** This pack is 30 shatterproof ornaments, each 2.36 inches wide, in champagne gold, in five finishes: glitter, faceted, confetti, gold thread and woven wire. Mixing finishes inside one colour is what gives a tree depth, and the storage tube keeps them for next year. 4.7 stars from 916.

**The porch, and a pair of trees around the door.** This set is 2 faux autumn trees, each 5 feet tall, with copper, rust and amber leaves on a trunk that splits in two for storage, standing in black pots with dried moss. One tree at each side of a door frames it better than a single large pot, and the same pair works on a hallway or beside a fireplace. 4.4 stars from 147.

Start with the porch and the window if you only have an hour, and leave the tree for the weekend after Halloween. Everything on this list works for more than one of the three, so nothing has to go back into a box on November 1.
