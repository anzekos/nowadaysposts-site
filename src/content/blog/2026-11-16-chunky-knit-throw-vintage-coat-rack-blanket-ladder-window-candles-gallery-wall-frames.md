The five home decor topics people are saving most on Pinterest this week come down to one idea: make the house feel warmer without a big purchase. Cozy home decor, a vintage entryway, blanket storage, Christmas window lights and budget decorating all point to small pieces that change a room in an afternoon. Here is one answer to each.

**A chunky knit throw for the sofa or the bed.** A 50 by 60 inch throw in cream chenille yarn with a thick open loop knit that looks handmade. It drapes in soft deep folds, so over the arm of a sofa, the end of a bed or the back of an accent chair it adds texture to a neutral room straight away. Cream is the safest color if your walls and sofa are already beige or grey.

**A vintage coat rack for the entryway.** Vintage entryway furniture is trending, but you do not need a big bench or a cabinet for the look. This is a 34 inch whitewashed wood board with 10 black tri-hooks, each with a tall center prong and two short side prongs, so a coat, a bag and a hat do not pile on one hook. It suits a narrow hallway, a mudroom or the wall behind a bedroom door. Find the studs or use proper anchors, because ten hooks full of winter coats are heavy.

**A blanket ladder for blanket storage.** A black wood ladder with 6 rungs, 70 inches tall and about 17 inches wide at the base, that leans against the wall. Throws, quilts and bath towels hang over the rungs in plain sight, so you grab one without digging through a basket. It also takes the chunky throw above when you are not using it. Check the ceiling height and leave a little room at the top.

**Christmas window candles.** A pack of 10 flameless LED tapers, about 10 inches tall, that stick to the glass with suction cups or stand in the included gold holders. A remote controls the timer and the dimmer, so the window glows at dusk without anyone lighting anything, which is safer around curtains and kids. Put the candles at different heights on one window for the best look from the street.

**A gallery wall frame set.** 19 black frames in three sizes, 3 for 8x10, 8 for 5x7 and 8 for 4x6 photos, with mats and a hanger or a stand on each. One box fills a blank sofa wall or bed wall, which is why it fits the budget home decorating idea. Lay the frames on the floor first and cut paper templates, so you hang them once instead of making extra holes.

If you only add one thing before the holidays, make it the throw or the window candles, since both change a room the same day they arrive. The coat rack and the ladder are the practical ones that keep working in January, when the Christmas decor goes back in the box.
