The first Christmas boxes are coming out of the closet, and Pinterest searches show it. Red and white Christmas tree ideas are up 300% this month, "christmas table decor ideas" is up 400%, and people are already asking how to refresh a bedroom and an entryway before guests arrive. Here is one find for each.

**A red and white tree without buying twelve boxes.** One box of 34 shatterproof ornaments in glitter, matte, clear glass look, mesh and berry-filled finishes, from 1.6 to 3.5 inches. The mix matters: a tree that uses five different finishes of the same two colours looks collected rather than bought in one go. Because they are plastic, the same box also fills a bowl, a garland or a mantel without worry around kids and pets.

**Christmas decor that fits on one tray.** A set of 8 pieces: a plush gnome in a red hat, a wooden countdown train, a wreath with a bow, a Merry Christmas sign, a boot vase with faux greenery, a Santa sign and a bead garland. Put them on a tray on the coffee table, along a mantel or on a kitchen shelf. It is the quickest way to get a farmhouse Christmas look in a room that has no space for a second tree.

**Old money Christmas, in two sizes.** Two gold resin tree figurines, 8.7 and 5.6 inches tall, with a layered carved shape and a gold leaf finish. No plug, no batteries. Set them on a console table or a bookshelf next to a candle and they catch the light the way much more expensive pieces do.

**The entryway fix that is not Christmas.** A 3 tier bamboo shoe bench, 27.6 inches wide. The slatted top seats up to 286 lbs, so you can sit down to put your shoes on, and the two shelves under it hold 3 pairs each. The slats let wet shoes dry, which is useful the moment boots come out for winter. It works by the front door, in a mudroom or at the foot of a bed.

**A bedroom refresh in one box.** A set of two 20.75 inch ceramic table lamps with a ribbed cream glaze, brushed gold poles and linen drum shades. The shade softens the light to a warm glow, and a matched pair on two nightstands makes the whole bed wall look planned. They also work as a console pair in a hallway or living room.

If you only do one thing this weekend, start with the ornaments or the tray, because Christmas pins saved now are the ones people are still opening in December. The shoe bench and the lamps are the two that stay useful long after the decorations go back in the box.
