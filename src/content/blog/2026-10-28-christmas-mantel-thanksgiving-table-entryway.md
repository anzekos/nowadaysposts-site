Halloween is three days out, and the next two holidays are already moving. Christmas decorations 2026 is up 300% on Pinterest this month, Thanksgiving table decor is up 300%, and Christmas table decor ideas is up 300% too. Here are five finds: three for the holidays and two that earn their space all year.

**The mantel, finished in one step.** A pre-lit Christmas garland with lights is the fastest way to dress a fireplace. This 9 ft snowy pinecone garland has warm white LEDs, frosted tips and a built-in timer. Drape it across a mantel, a banister or a doorway.

**The Thanksgiving table, already styled.** A fall table centerpiece that comes arranged: maple leaves, berries, mini pumpkins, pinecones and three flameless LED candles on an iron base. Set it down the middle of the dining table or on a sideboard.

**The Christmas table, covered in one go.** Seven pieces of red buffalo plaid with ornaments, pine and gold stars: a 72 inch runner and six placemats. It works for a holiday dinner or a plain weeknight at a farmhouse table.

**The hallway that never has enough hooks.** This entryway coat rack with shelf has seven double hooks for coats and bags, four key hooks and a top shelf for a vase or mail. It's wall mounted, so it uses no floor space in a small entry or mudroom.

**The gap beside the washer.** A slim rolling storage cart only 7.1 inches wide, with four mesh tiers, a wood top, a handle and lockable wheels. It slides into the gap between the washer and the wall, or beside a bathroom vanity.

If you only do one thing this week, hang the garland. It takes ten minutes, and it makes the room feel like the holidays before the tree is even up.
